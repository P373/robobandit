LUCKY LEO

☘️ King Grumbles stole Leo's pot of gold! Run, jump and stomp across the Emerald Isle to get it back.
  1. Run and jump: ← → to walk, Space (or ↑ / Z) to jump. Hold X or Shift to run.◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, hold B to run. 🎮 Ⓐ jumps, Ⓧ runs. Hold jump to go higher!
  2. Stomp the goblins. Stomp a snail and kick its shell to knock out a whole row! Don't stomp the spiky hedgehogs.
  3. Bump the ☘️ blocks from below: a four-leaf clover makes Leo big, a rainbow flower lets him throw rainbow sparks (XB), and a lucky horseshoe makes him unstoppable.
  4. Grab gold coins (100 = an extra life) and the 5 rainbow gems hidden in every level.
  5. Break the rainbow tape at the end. The higher you hit it, the more stars! Somewhere there's a golden key to a secret level… 🤫

THE GOLD IS BACK!

🌈 King Grumbles is beaten and Leo has his pot of gold. Hooray!
