LUCKY LEO

☘️ King Grumbles stole Leo's pot of gold! Run, jump and stomp across the Emerald Isle, then over the sea to the Frosty Isle, to get it all back.
  1. Run and jump: ← → to walk, Space (or Z) to jump. Hold X or Shift to run.◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, hold B to run. 🎮 Ⓐ jumps, Ⓧ runs. Hold jump to go higher!
  2. Stomp the goblins. Stomp a snail and kick its shell to knock out a whole row! Or hold run as you walk into a shell to pick it up, and let go to throw it. Springs can be carried the same way: move them wherever you need a big bounce! Don't stomp the spiky hedgehogs… unless you ground pound them. An extra power-up waits in the box at the top: tap it (or press Q 🎮 LB) to drop it when you need it.
  3. 🎩 Throw your hat (C, 🎮 Ⓨ, 🎩 button) to knock out enemies and grab coins. It comes back like a boomerang, and you can bounce off it in mid-air: throw it, keep holding the hat button so it hovers, then jump and land on it (hold jump to bounce higher, then spin for a little more)! Hold up (↑▲, 🎮 up) to look up and throw it straight up into blocks, or crouch to throw it low at little enemies.
  4. 🦄 Shamrock the unicorn: hit the special block and a cloud shines a rainbow down, and there he is! Hop on! Jump higher, hold jump to float, stomp even the spiky hedgehogs, and press run to fire a rainbow laser from his eyes that turns enemies into coins. If you get hit you just fall off: catch him again! ↓ + jump hops off.
  5. ⬇️ Ground pound: press ↓▼ (🎮 LT) in the air. High jump: hold ↓▼ (🎮 LT) and jump for a big backflip. 🌀 Spin: press jump again in the air to twirl a little higher (once a jump).
  6. Bump the ☘️ blocks from below: a four-leaf clover makes Leo big, a rainbow flower lets him throw rainbow sparks (XB), and a lucky horseshoe makes him unstoppable.
  7. Grab gold coins (100 = an extra life) and the 5 rainbow gems hidden in every level.
  8. Break the rainbow tape at the end. The higher you hit it, the more stars! Somewhere there's a golden key to a secret level… and a secret exit for anyone who can find another way to the end. 🤫

THE GOLD IS BACK!

🌈 King Grumbles is beaten and Leo has his pot of gold. Hooray!
