PUMPKIN PALS

🎃 It's Halloween night, and the grumpy old scarecrow has scattered the little pumplings all over town! Help Patch find every one and bring them home in the pumpkin wagon.
  1. Run and jump: A D to walk (keep going and you'll speed up), Space to jump. Hold Shift to run.◀ ▶ to walk (keep going and you'll speed up), A to jump. 🎮 Ⓐ jumps, hold Ⓧ to run. Hold jump to go higher, and press it again in the air to spin a little higher!
  2. Find the lost pumplings. Touch one and it joins your line, bouncing along behind you. Pumplings love candy corn… and talking nonsense. 🍬
  3. Throw a pumpling (J, 🎮 RT, the 🎃 button) to bonk bats, cats and skeletons, smash crates and bump blocks. Hold up (W▲, 🎮 stick up) to throw straight up. Don't worry: they always run back to you!
  4. 👻 Ghosts can't be stomped! They sneak up when you look away and hide when you look at them. Turn and face them, then throw a pumpling to say BOO!
  5. 💥 Get hit and a few pumplings scatter. Quick, grab them back! You only lose a life if you get hit with nobody left in your line.
  6. ⬇️ Ground pound: press S▼ (🎮 LT) in the air. It smashes crates and knocks out skeletons for good.
  7. 🍬 Grab candy corn (100 = an extra life), the 3 golden candies in every level, and the 🌕 moon candy that makes you glow and unstoppable.
  8. 🛒 Reach the pumpkin wagon at the end. Every pumpling you bring home is worth points!

HAPPY HALLOWEEN!

🎃 The scarecrow is beaten and every pumpling is safe at home. Boo-ya!
