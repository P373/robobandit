MANIFEST DESTINY

🇺🇸 America's story, from the Pilgrims at Plimoth in 1620 to the railroad that joined the country coast to coast. Run, jump and learn a little history in every state!
📖 How to play
  1. Run and jump: A D to walk, Space to jump. Hold Shift to run.◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, hold B to run. 🎮 A jumps, X runs. Hold jump to go higher!
  2. 🦃 Stomp the animals in your way: wild turkeys (watch out: they fan their tails and charge!), wolves, crows, the Mayflower's rats and the redcoats. Stomp a box turtle and kick its shell, or hold run to pick the shell up and throw it. Porcupines are prickly: only a ground pound works on them.
  3. ⬇️ Ground pound: press S▼ (🎮 LT) in the air. High jump: hold S▼ and jump. 🌀 Spin: press jump again in the air. 🧱 Wall kick: slide down a wall and jump off it.
  4. 🎩 Find the hat with the red band and you can throw your hat like a boomerang (J · 🎮 Y · 🎩 button). Keep holding the button and it hovers: jump on it for an extra boost!
  5. Bump the ? blocks from below for coins and power-ups: a 🎃 pumpkin makes you big, a 🍎 basket of apples lets you throw apples (ShiftB · 🎮 X), and a lucky horseshoe makes you unstoppable for a few seconds. 100 coins (Pine Tree shillings!) is an extra life.
  6. 🌽 Find the 5 ears of corn hidden in every New England level. Corn kept the Pilgrims alive! Each region has its own treasures: in the Mid-Atlantic, 🪶 quill pens, like the ones the Declaration of Independence was written with.
  7. 📜 Every level starts with a bit of the story. Bump the info boxes (a 📖 book in New England, a 📰 bundle of newspapers from the Mid-Atlantic on) and your hero tells you a piece of history. After each region's boss comes a quiz on what you learned!
  8. 🗺️ The map is the whole United States, with Canada and Mexico next door. Press M · 🎮 X · the 🗺️ button, or pinch to see it all. Signposts 🚧 mark levels still being built. Look for secret exits: they lead to places you can't reach any other way. 🤫
  9. 👪 The heroes: you're Wrestling Brewster, a real Pilgrim boy who sailed on the Mayflower when he was about six. A new real-life hero, with a new skill, joins you in every region: after New England, Sybil Ludington, the fastest runner and the best rider on Star the horse. Pick your hero in 👪 The heroes.
  10. 🚂 The railroad: find a region's hidden golden spike and its railroad depot opens. Ride the train between open depots, and try each depot's Railroad Challenge: the hardest levels in the game!
