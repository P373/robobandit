MANIFEST DESTINY

🇺🇸 America's story, from the Pilgrims at Plimoth in 1620 to the railroad that joined the country coast to coast. Run, jump and learn a little history in every state!
  1. Run and jump: A D to walk, Space to jump. Hold Shift to run.◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, hold B to run. 🎮 A jumps, X runs. Hold jump to go higher!
  2. 🦃 Stomp the animals in your way: wild turkeys (watch out: they fan their tails and charge!), wolves, crows and the Mayflower's rats. Stomp a box turtle and kick its shell, or hold run to pick the shell up and throw it. Porcupines are prickly: only a ground pound works on them.
  3. ⬇️ Ground pound: press S▼ (🎮 LT) in the air. High jump: hold S▼ and jump. 🌀 Spin: press jump again in the air. 🧱 Wall kick: slide down a wall and jump off it.
  4. 🎩 Find the hat with the red band and you can throw your hat like a boomerang (J · 🎮 Y · 🎩 button). Keep holding the button and it hovers: jump on it for an extra boost!
  5. Bump the ? blocks from below for coins and power-ups. 100 coins (Pine Tree shillings!) is an extra life.
  6. 🌽 Find the 5 ears of corn hidden in every New England level. Corn kept the Pilgrims alive! Each region has its own treasures to find.
  7. 📜 Every level starts with a bit of the story, and every win ends with a "Did you know?" fact. Win again for a new one.
  8. 🗺️ The map is the whole United States, with Canada and Mexico next door. Press M · 🎮 X · the 🗺️ button, or pinch to see it all. Signposts 🚧 mark levels still being built. Look for secret exits: they lead to places you can't reach any other way. 🤫
  9. 👪 The Hart family: you're Will Hart, a Pilgrim boy. A new member of the family, with a new skill, joins you in every region.
