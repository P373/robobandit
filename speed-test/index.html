How does it work?

Ping: sends 15 tiny requests and times each round trip. The middle (median) time is your ping; how much it wobbles is the jitter.

Download: pulls big chunks of random data over 4 connections at once for about 8 seconds, counting every byte as it arrives. The first second is skipped while the connection warms up. Speed = bits received ÷ seconds.

Upload: the same, but sending chunks of data up instead.

The test data comes from Cloudflare's free public speed-test server (speed.cloudflare.com), the nearest one to you. A full test uses roughly 50–500 MB, depending on how fast your connection is.
