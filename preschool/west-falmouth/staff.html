Teaching staff

Our teachers

CB

Christina Brousseau

Director / Lead Teacher

Christina has over 12 years of teaching experience, the last 8 at the preschool level, plus many years working with kindergarten, first and second grade classes. She also has experience in the ASD classroom and in sub-separate social emotional and learning center classrooms.

She earned a bachelor's degree in Sociology with a minor in Elementary Education from Bridgewater State University, and a Master's degree in Early Childhood Education from American International College. When she is not in the classroom you can find her at the beach, spending time with her family or reading.

SO

Sheri O’Malley

Teacher

After receiving her BFA with a minor in Education at UMass Amherst, Mrs. O'Malley moved to Maryland, where she founded and developed a kindergarten enrichment program for the Montgomery County Schools. She also taught kindergarten in Maine, then moved to Falmouth, where she began teaching on the Cape at Teaticket Elementary School, first as a substitute teacher and then specializing in MCAS tutoring. She has been part of the West Falmouth Preschool community since 2008.

BO

Brenna Oberton

Teacher

Brenna has over 15 years of experience teaching and caring for children in early childhood settings, including preschools, kindergarten, day cares, camps and as a nanny. She is EEC Lead Teacher qualified and earned an Associate's degree in Early Childhood Education.

She is also a mom to four children, and a West Falmouth Preschool alum! Her favorite hobby is reading, and she loves reading books to the preschoolers and helping them learn the pre-literacy skills that will someday help them enjoy reading on their own.

SR

Sarah Richards

Music and Movement Teacher

Sarah has her Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Music Education from the University of Massachusetts Amherst, and certifications in Orff Schulwerk (levels 1, 2 & 3), Conversational Solfège (1 & 2) and two early childhood music programs (First Steps in Music, Music Together).

Before moving to the Cape, she taught general music and chorus in the Berlin/Boylston Public Schools for ten years, founding two musical theater programs. A flutist and singer, she sang with the internationally competing Millennium Magic Chorus, and now sings at Cape Cod Church. She shares her passion for music with her own children and the West Falmouth Preschool community.

Board members

Our board of directors

Many of our board members are former WFPS parents (and even former students!) who stay involved to keep the little red schoolhouse thriving.

SG

Sara Giguere

Sara lives in Falmouth with her husband, Caleb, and their four kids. Their youngest was part of the West Falmouth Preschool community from 2022 to 2024, shortly after the family moved to Cape Cod. Joining the board is one way Sara hopes to keep the preschool a special place for future families.

Sara is CFO and partner at Murray & MacDonald Insurance, where she has worked for nearly 20 years, and serves on the board of the Massachusetts Association of Insurance Agents.

SM

Sydney McCabe

Sydney joined WFPS in the fall of 2003, when the school was still in the basement of the West Falmouth Library. She was part of the fundraising committee whose Spring 2004 event began the "Raise the Roof" campaign, helped open the schoolhouse for the 2005 school year, and served as President from Fall 2004 to Spring 2006. Since 2006 she has served on the board of directors as a guide in all things financial.

MS

Megan Stimpson

Megan was a student at West Falmouth Preschool herself, when her mom, "Mrs. Phillips", was the teacher. Her own children attended from 2017 to 2021, and she served as President of the Executive Committee for three of those four years.

She earned a B.S. in Mathematics from Bridgewater State University and an MBA from UMass Boston, worked 14 years at BNY Mellon, and is now heavily involved in North Falmouth Elementary's PTO. She joined the Board of Directors in the 2023/2024 school year.

KT

Kristin Tarantino

Kristin's two boys attended WFPS between 2013 and 2017, when she served as Registrar, Publicity Coordinator and, in her youngest son's final year, President. She has been a board member ever since; her love for West Falmouth's little red schoolhouse continues to drive her to take an active role in this special community of parents and teachers.

JE

Joan Eisenberg

Joan has been involved in education for most of her career. While earning an MS in Early Childhood Education at Bank Street College of Education, she opened and ran a preschool with fellow students; she later taught on the Zuni Pueblo in New Mexico, opened the Allen Avenue Children's Center in Falmouth, and was a children's librarian in Cambridge and at Milton Academy's Lower School. Now back on the Cape, she is excited to be involved in early childhood education once again.
