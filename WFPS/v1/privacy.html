Privacy Policy

How West Falmouth Preschool collects, uses and protects your information.

Introduction

West Falmouth Preschool is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our community members' personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information, including information collected through our website, during registration, and as part of our educational programs.

Information collection

We collect information from and about our users in various ways, including:

  • Website navigation: information on how you use and interact with our website.
  • Registration and enrollment: personal details provided during the registration and enrollment process, such as child and parent names, addresses, and contact information.
  • Communication: information received through email communications, inquiries, and parent feedback.

Use of information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our educational services.
  • Communicate with you, including sending school updates, information about events, and responses to inquiries.
  • Improve our website and services by understanding how they are used.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. However, we may share it with third-party service providers under strict confidentiality agreements to perform services on our behalf. Additionally, we may disclose information if required by law.

Data security

We implement various security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information, including secure servers and protected databases. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important. Therefore, we never collect or maintain information on our website from those we know are under 13, and no part of our website is structured to attract anyone under 13.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically for any changes.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch with us at wfpsregistrar@gmail.com.
