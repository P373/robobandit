Program overview

Three, four or five days a week

West Falmouth Preschool offers families a three, four or five-day preschool program. A child must be 3 years old by September 1 to enroll.

The children attend school from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. each day, with lunch, and an option to "Stay and Play" until 3:00 p.m. The school day consists of a flexible curriculum and regular enrichments.

Our classroom is a mixture of age groups, and the students' work is determined by their capability. We strive for kindergarten readiness, and students are encouraged to achieve or surpass their individual academic goals. Children can choose from many activities, including arts and crafts, shapes and colors, dramatic and imaginary play, outside play and reading.

Stay & Play, 1:00 to 3:00

Stay and Play is an extension of our Morning Program and focuses mainly on enhancing social development. When the weather is nice, much of the time is spent playing outdoors on our well-equipped, recently updated, fully-fenced playground.

The children play with their peers, have a snack and take a 20 to 30-minute rest. It's a wonderful opportunity for your child to form stronger bonds with their teachers and classmates. Stay and Play is available on your child's enrolled school days.

Curriculum

"Exposure" is the keyword

We offer a flexible curriculum. Our teachers focus on the whole child: communication skills; physical, social/emotional and cognitive development; and strong self-care skills. The daily routine includes a nice balance of creative free play, teacher-guided projects and organized group activities.

Younger children's group activities focus mainly on social interactions, developing independence and feeling secure in their first preschool experience. For older children, we continue to promote social and emotional growth while also focusing on reading and basic math skills, and on fine motor skills such as writing and drawing. Our goal is to help guide each child to meet their individual developmental needs and prepare for kindergarten.

Enrichments

Monthly programs include Music and Movement, Yoga and Spanish, and we make regular walking trips to the West Falmouth Library for story time and crafts.

Outside specialists bring hands-on experiences about Cape Cod and surrounding communities, diverse cultures, and personal health and safety. In the fall, staff from the Wampanoag Tribe share what life was like for Native Americans, and we visit Bourne Farm with families. In October (National Fire Prevention Month) we visit the local fire station, and in February (National Children's Dental Health Month) a dental hygienist visits. Some years bring extras like the Mad Scientist and Barnyard Babies!

Teachers also weave in music and movement, story time, seasonal themes and an in-house lending library. Our fully fenced playground, recently remodeled with new equipment and turf, is used every day all year round.
