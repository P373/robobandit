Registration

Joining our school

We encourage parents to meet with the program administrator before a child is admitted, and we offer children and parents an orientation to the program. We'll ask about your family's interests and needs, support your child's transition, and coordinate with you on any other services or educational needs your child receives.

Once enrolled, we talk through each child's developmental history with parents every year and keep it in the child's permanent record.

Toilet training is not an eligibility requirement. West Falmouth Preschool does not and shall not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion (creed), gender, gender expression, age, national origin (ancestry), disability, marital status, sexual orientation, or military status, in any of its activities or operations.

Good to know

  • Tuition is due by the first of the month. A $25 late fee applies after the 10th, rising to $50 at 20 days late. At 30 days late, enrollment may be reviewed by the Executive Committee and Board.
  • There is also a $50 monthly cleaning fee.
  • See the parent handbook for complete enrollment policies.

Tuition Assistance Program

Funds raised by our families support a Tuition Assistance Program. Families interested in tuition assistance complete an application and provide some financial information to the school director, in confidence.

Please contact the school for how to pay.
