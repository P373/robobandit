Privacy Policy

How West Falmouth Preschool collects, uses and protects your information.

Suggested update: this version matches a simple website with no cookies and no database. The board should review it before it's used.

Introduction

West Falmouth Preschool is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our community members' personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and safeguard information, including through our website, during registration, and as part of our educational programs.

Information we collect

  • Website visits: we use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Registration and enrollment: details you give us when registering, such as child and parent names, addresses and contact information.
  • Messages: information you send us by email or through our inquiry form.

How we use it

  • To provide and run our educational program.
  • To communicate with you, including school updates, events and answers to your questions.
  • To improve our website and services.

Sharing

We never sell or rent personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us run the school, under confidentiality agreements, or when required by law.

Security

Enrollment records are kept by the school, not on this website, and access is limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission over the Internet is entirely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

We never collect information on our website from anyone we know is under 13, and no part of our website is designed to attract children under 13. We only post photos of children with their parents' written permission.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; please check back periodically.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email wfpsregistrar@gmail.com.
