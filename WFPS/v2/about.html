Our mission

A love of learning, independence and creativity

West Falmouth Preschool is a parent cooperative that nurtures and develops a child's love of learning, independence and creativity, through a positive learning environment and a flexible, semi-structured curriculum that builds self-confidence and gets children ready for kindergarten.

We are licensed by the Massachusetts Department of Early Education and Care and accredited by the National Academy of Early Childhood Programs. Every teacher holds a degree in education, is qualified by the EEC, and is certified in pediatric first aid and CPR.

How our co-op is run

The Executive Committee and the Director make day-to-day decisions. Each year parents elect five officers (President, Vice President, Treasurer and two Secretaries), who appoint up to six more roles, from Registrar to Fundraising Coordinator. Monthly Executive meetings are open to everyone.

Our history

A schoolhouse built by a community

For many years the preschool met in the basement of the West Falmouth Library. In 2002, Director Elizabeth Ghiorse Osgood and builder Steve Good, a WFPS parent, walked the land on Blacksmith Shop Road and decided a schoolhouse of our own could be built.

The "Raising the Roof after 50 years" campaign raised about $200,000 in under 18 months. Good Builders, Inc. built the school in 2004–2005, Upper Cape Tech students built the cupola, and Falmouth Engineers, Inc. did the site work. The school is named for John and Ruth Ghiorse, lifelong educators, and the antique brass bell in the cupola still rings today.

WFPS alumni

Generations of West Falmouth children have started school here, and some come back as parents, teachers and board members. One of our teachers and one of our board members are WFPS alums themselves!
