Being part of a co-op

What we ask of families each year

Our school runs because families pitch in. Here's the whole commitment, up front:

1 dayhelping in the classroom, 9:00–1:00
$200raised per child for the main annual fundraiser, plus helping organize it
2clean-ups, in the fall and the spring (kids welcome!)
3parent meetings: September, January and May

Non-graduating families also help host graduation. Parents may occasionally be asked to help with small repairs, snow removal, enrichments, or to sub when a teacher is out.

Questions

Frequently asked

What ages do you serve?

Children ages 3 to 5. Your child must be 3 years old by September 1 of the school year.

Does my child need to be toilet trained?

No. Toilet training is not an eligibility requirement.

What does the co-op ask of parents?

One classroom day (9:00–1:00) a year, raising $200 per child for the main annual fundraiser and helping organize it, the fall and spring clean-ups, three parent meetings (September, January and May), and helping host graduation in a non-graduating year. Officers and appointed board members are exempt from clean-ups.

Can I choose which days my child attends?

Yes. Choose a three, four or five-day plan, each with or without Stay & Play until 3:00.

Is help with tuition available?

Yes. Our Tuition Assistance Program is funded by our families' fundraising. Complete a short application and share financial information with the director, in confidence.

What will my child do all day?

Free play centers, morning meeting, snack, outside play, a special (music, yoga, Spanish or the West Falmouth Library), a weekly theme activity and lunch. See a day at WFPS.

Is the school licensed?

Yes, by the Massachusetts Department of Early Education and Care, and accredited by the National Academy of Early Childhood Programs.
