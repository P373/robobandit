Why a co-op?

Why families choose West Falmouth Preschool

Everyone knows your child

A small mixed-age classroom where certified teachers and parents work side by side. Children see familiar faces every day.

Families are part of it

Help in the classroom one day a year, join the clean-ups and the annual fundraiser, and get to know the other families. Lifelong friendships start here.

Ready for kindergarten

A flexible curriculum where "exposure" is the keyword: art, music, math, reading readiness and science, with weekly themes and monthly music, yoga and Spanish.

Our programs

Mornings of learning, afternoons of play

Morning Program, 9:00–1:00

Choose three, four or five days a week. A day of free play centers, morning meeting, outside play, a special (music, yoga, Spanish or the library), a theme activity and lunch together.

See a day at WFPS →

Stay & Play, 1:00–3:00

An afternoon extension focused on social development: rest, snack and lots of outdoor play on our fully fenced, recently updated playground.
