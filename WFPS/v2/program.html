Program overview

Three, four or five days a week

Children attend from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. with lunch, and can Stay and Play until 3:00 p.m. A child must be 3 years old by September 1 to enroll.

Our classroom is a mix of ages, and each child's work matches what they're ready for. We strive for kindergarten readiness, and children are encouraged to reach or go beyond their own goals. They choose from arts and crafts, shapes and colors, dramatic and imaginary play, outside play, reading and more.

Stay & Play, 1:00 to 3:00

An extension of the Morning Program that focuses on social development. Children play with friends, have a snack and take a 20 to 30-minute rest, and when the weather is nice they spend much of the time on our fully fenced, recently updated playground. Available on your child's enrolled days.

Curriculum

"Exposure" is the keyword

Our teachers focus on the whole child: communication skills; physical, social/emotional and cognitive development; and strong self-care skills, with a balance of creative free play, teacher-guided projects and group activities.

For younger children, the focus is friendships, independence and feeling secure in their first preschool experience. Older children keep growing socially and emotionally while also working on reading, basic math and fine motor skills like writing and drawing.

Enrichments through the year

  • Every month: Music and Movement, Yoga and Spanish
  • Regularly: walks to the West Falmouth Library for story time and crafts
  • Fall: visitors from the Wampanoag Tribe, and a family trip to Bourne Farm
  • October: a visit to the fire station for Fire Prevention Month
  • February: a dental hygienist visits for Children's Dental Health Month
  • Some years: extras like the Mad Scientist and Barnyard Babies
