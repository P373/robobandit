Specifications

  • Pages: 12, one per month (January–December 2027), portrait.
  • Page size: 16 × 22 in trimmed. Each page: the print on top, the month grid below.
  • Paper: cream or natural, uncoated or matte, heavy enough to hang flat.
  • Format of the original (photos below): sheets 16 in wide and about 44 in long, two 22 in pages per sheet printed head to head, with a die-cut hanging slot at the centre fold.
  • Colour: full colour; the red rules and holiday dates are a single deep red.
