Size and construction

Finished size16″ wide × 22″ tall (portrait)
Flat sheet16″ × 44″, folded in half at the top
Construction3 folded sheets × 4 pages = 12 months, nested at the fold (not bound: see below)
CoverOptional: please quote with and without
FallbackIf 44″ isn’t possible: 6 separate 16 × 22 sheets, bound across the top

Printing

ColorFull color both sides (4/4, CMYK process)
MethodDigital for small runs or offset for large: your recommendation
Back sidePrinted so each month reads upright when the page is flipped up (head-to-head or head-to-foot: please confirm)

Paper

Weight70–80# text, uncoated, cream or natural (the original was 70# cream text)
Upgrade option100# text, if the fold is scored
Cover, if used80# cover, same color
Please tell usWhich cream or natural stocks you carry, and whether show-through will be a problem

Binding and finishing

BindingNone. The 3 folded sheets are nested, loose, with no staples
FoldingFolded at 22″. Optional: score the folds first so they don’t crack
Slot in the foldA die-cut slot at the center of each sheet’s fold line
Backing board16 × 22 heavy cream cover stock, with a tab sticking up from the center of its top edge
AssemblyThe folded sheets sit over the backing board, with the tab passing up through the slots. The tab holds the sheets in place and the board keeps the calendar flat

Files

FormatPDF/X-1a:2001, 300 ppi, no fonts (all type drawn), no transparency
Color modeCMYK, GRACoL 2013 Uncoated (CGATS21-2 RCU); black type 100% K, overprinting; max ink 278%
Bleed and margins⅛″ bleed, crop marks outside the bleed, TrimBox and BleedBox set; nothing within 0.375″ of the trim
Layout13 single 16 × 22 pages in reading order (cover, January–December); you impose the 16 × 44 sheets
