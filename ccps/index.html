Questions

Good to know

Can I drop off a package that already has a label?

Yes, and it's free, as long as it's labeled, taped and ready to go. For large or heavy packages, call first with the dimensions and weight. Please note we don't have QR code scanners for return labels yet, so bring a printed label.

When does my package leave?

FedEx Ground is picked up at 3:00 pm, FedEx Express at 4:30 pm, and UPS Ground and Air at 5:00 pm, every weekday. Get it to us before then for same-day pickup.

Can you ship lobsters or fish?

Yes! We have shipping coolers of all sizes and frozen gel packs. Bring your seafood and we'll handle the rest. Overnight is best for anything perishable.

Can you pack valuable or fragile things?

That's what we do best: artwork, antiques, glass, china and heirlooms. We'll build a custom box if nothing else fits, and you can add insurance.

Is there anything you can't ship?

Hazardous materials such as fuel, paint, aerosols and some batteries have special rules or can't be shipped from here. If you're not sure, call us before you bring it in.

Are you open on Saturdays?

No. We're open Monday to Friday, 9 to 5, year round, and closed on weekends and major holidays.
