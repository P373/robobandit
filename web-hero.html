WEB HERO

🕸️ Swing across the city and stop the Bandit Bots!
  1. Hold Space or the mouseyour finger anywhere on the screen to shoot a web and swing.
  2. Let go to fly through the air!
  3. Crash into Bandit Bots to bonk them. Bonk lots in a row without landing for a big combo.
  4. Land on rooftops to run. Don't fall to the street! You have 3 hearts.
