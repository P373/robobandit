Witch Way Out

🏁 The Moonlight Grand Prix! Race the other witches to the moon… and stay ahead of the Great Pumpkin 🎃
  1. Fly your broomstick anywhere: A D turn, W climb, S dive. put your thumb down and drag to steer.
  2. 🎩 Throw your hat (🎮 B or J, 🎩 button) at the witches ahead to bonk them off their brooms! It flies back to you like a boomerang.
  3. ✨ Boost with magic dust (hold 🎮 RT or X, or Shift, ✨ button). The meter fills up again, faster through the glowing rings.
  4. 🪂 Air brake to slow down (hold 🎮 LT, or Ctrl, 🪂 button). It costs no magic.
  5. ❔ Grab the magic boxes for a power-up, then use it (🎮 Y, F, ❔ button): 🍬 rocket · 🦇 bats chase the leader · 🧪 boost refill · 👻 ghost cloak · 🎩 triple hat · 🕸️ web trap.
  6. 🛡️ Shield (🎮 A, Space, 🛡️ button) blocks lightning in the storm and hats thrown at you.
  7. Look around with the 🎮 right stick, Z / X or a mouse drag or a second finger. LB / RB or click the right stick (or R) swings back behind you.
  8. 👥 Up to four players! Press 2, 3 or 4 and the screen splits. Each player uses their own 🎮 controller (the first one plugged in is Player 1), and Players 1 and 2 can share the keyboard: Player 1 WASD fly, left Shift boost, left Ctrl brake, Space shield, G hat, F power-up · Player 2 IJKL fly, right Shift boost, right Ctrl brake, Enter shield, O hat, U power-up. Your hats and power-ups can bonk each other too!
  9. ⚡ Fly through the glowing arrows for a dash of speed. 🚀 Rocket start: start holding boost as the 2 shows (not before!). 💨 Slipstream: tuck in right behind another racer to shoot past.
  10. ✂️ Find the shortcuts! The arch through the rock spire, the updraft up to the ring highway, the old barn, and the haunted mansion's front door.
  11. Off the castle battlements, down Lantern Canyon, over Thunder Peaks, along Hollow Village, through the graveyard, past the Haunted Mansion and across Moonlight Lake. Mind the windmill, the twisters, the stomping tombstones and the chomping pumpkin gates, or the pumpkin catches up!
