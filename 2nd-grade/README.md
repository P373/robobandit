# 2nd grade

Lessons and learning games for 2nd graders, following a typical 2nd grade year: math, language arts, science and
social studies. `index.html` is the folder page (linked from the home page); each subject is one page here, built on
the shared lesson engine in `../lesson.js` and `../lesson.css` (the same one the 5th grade uses):

| Page | Lessons | Games |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `math.html` | Place value to 1,000 · Add & subtract · Money & time · Measure & graph · Shapes & equal groups | Fact Rocket · Coin Shop |
| `reading.html` | Retell a story · Main idea · Compare & contrast · Predict & infer · Author's purpose · Spelling patterns | Word Builder · Story Order |
| `science.html` | Erosion & landforms · States of matter · Habitats & adaptations · Engineering design | Matter Sorter · Habitat Match |
| `social-studies.html` | Being a good citizen · Families & traditions · Famous Americans · Maps & globes · Consumers & producers | Map Quest · Market Day |

Every lesson starts with **what is it?** and **why do we care?**, then explains the ideas one slide at a time,
each with an animated picture, and ends with a quiz. The words are written for 7- and 8-year-olds, and every slide
has a 🔈 **Read to me** button. To add a subject, copy one of these pages, then add it to the `SUBJECTS` list in
`index.html` and to the 2nd grade list in `tests/run.js`.
