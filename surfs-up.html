HOW TO SURF

  1. You ride across the wave from left to right. The white, crashing curl behind you is chasing you. Don't let it catch you!
  2. Steer your board. ↑ tips the nose up the wave. ↓ tips it down. (W/S or ←/→ work too.) Put your thumb anywhere and a joystick appears. Drag up to tip the nose up the wave, drag down to tip it down. Drag further to turn harder.
  3. Speed comes from going down. Riding down the wave makes you faster. Riding up slows you down.
  4. Fly! Drop to the bottom, turn hard, and race straight up the wave. If you're fast enough, you shoot off the top into the air.
  5. Do a trick. In the air, keep holding ↑ or ↓the joystick up or down to spin. Go all the way around for a 360!
  6. Land it. Before you splash down, point your nose down the wave. Land pointing up and you wipe out.
  7. You get 60 seconds and 3 boards. Each wipeout costs a board. When the ride ends, five judges score you out of 10.
