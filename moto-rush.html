Moto Rush

🏍️ Three laps round the figure 8: over the bridge, under the bridge, through the barn… watch out for the cows! 🐄
  • 🏍️Ride: 🎮 RT gas, LT brake, left stick steers · ⌨️ W gas, S brake, A D steer · 👆 drag to steer, the bike goes by itself. Right stick, Z / X or a mouse drag looks all around.
  • 🌀Drift round the hairpins: hold B or Ctrl while you turn, and let go for a blue, then orange, mini-turbo.
  • 🔥Nitro (hold X or Shift). Fill it with wheelies (pull the stick back, or hold W + S), big air, drifting and tucking in behind a rider (slipstream).
  • ⤴️Hop (A or Space) over the cows, and press it again in mid-air off the ramps and the bridge for a trick: land it for a boost!
  • 🦵Kick the rider beside you (Y or K)! Ride over the gold arrows, find the shortcut through the red barn, and press gas as the 2 shows for a 🚀 rocket start.
  • 👥2, 3 or 4 players: a controller each. Players 1 and 2 can share the keyboard: P1 WASD, left Shift nitro, left Ctrl drift, Space hop, F kick · P2 IJKL, right Shift, right Ctrl, Enter hop, U kick.
